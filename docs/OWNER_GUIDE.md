# Website Manager: guide for the website owner

This is how to update the pjoutlawlegal.com website yourself. You do not
need any technical knowledge, and nothing you do here can break the design
of the site.

## How it works in one sentence

You edit a **draft**, look at the **preview**, and when you are happy you
**publish**. Visitors only ever see published content, and every published
version is kept, so you can always go back.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to **pjoutlawlegal.com/admin**.
2. Enter your e-mail and password.
3. If you have turned on two-step verification, enter the 6-digit code from
   your authenticator app.

**Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page
and follow the e-mail.

**Please turn on two-step verification** (Settings → Two-step verification).
It keeps the website safe even if someone learns your password. You will need
a free app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your
phone.

## 2. Changing text on a page

1. Click **Pages** in the menu on the left.
2. Click **Edit** next to the page.
3. Change the text in the boxes. Fields marked with a red * must be filled in.
4. Click **Save draft**. Your changes are now saved, but visitors do not see
   them yet.

Lists such as testimonials, services or the seven mediation steps can be
opened with the ▸ arrow. Use ↑ ↓ to change the order, 🗑 to remove an item,
and **Add …** at the bottom to add a new one.

If you leave the page before saving, your browser will warn you.

## 3. Changing a picture

1. In the editor, find the picture and click **Replace image**.
2. Choose one from the **Media library**, or **Upload new** from your
   computer (JPG, PNG or WebP photos up to 10 MB; large photos are made
   smaller automatically).
3. Fill in **Image description**: a short sentence saying what the picture
   shows (for example "Attorney Outlaw at her desk"). This helps visitors who
   use screen readers, and helps Google.
4. Click **Save draft**.

To go back to one of the website's original pictures, choose
**Original site images** in the same window.

## 4. Updating services

Click **Services** in the menu. It shows where services appear (Mediation
Services page, Practice Areas page and the Home page cards). Click **Edit**
to change them.

Please only describe services, credentials and results that are accurate and
current. Legal advertising rules apply to everything on the website.

## 5. Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

These are under **Pages** like any other page. They are legal text: have
Attorney Outlaw review every change before publishing, and update the date
line when you do. If the website starts collecting new information (for
example adding Google Analytics or a contact form), the Privacy Policy must
be updated first.

## 6. Phone number, e-mail, address and office hours

These appear in several places (header, footer, Contact page, call buttons),
so they are kept in one spot: **Settings → Business details**. Change them
once and they update everywhere.

## 7. Publishing

1. Open the page and click the **Preview** tab to check how it will look.
2. Click **Publish**.
3. Optionally type a short note about what changed (for example "Holiday
   hours"). The note appears in the history.
4. Click **Publish to the website**.

The live website updates in about **1 to 3 minutes**.

The coloured label at the top of each page tells you where things stand:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Original content | Not changed in the dashboard yet |
| Draft – not published | Saved, but visitors cannot see it |
| Unpublished changes | Visitors see an older version; publish to update |
| Published | Visitors see exactly what you see |

**Made a mistake?** Click **History** on the page, choose an earlier version,
click **Restore**, then **Publish**. If you have saved a draft but not
published it, **Discard draft** throws the draft away and goes back to what
is live.

## 8. Website traffic

**Analytics** will show visits, popular pages and where visitors come from.
It is **not connected yet**, so it says "Not connected" rather than showing
numbers. Once your developer connects Google Analytics, real figures will
appear there.

## 9. Inquiries

The website does not have a contact form; visitors call, fax or e-mail the
office. **Contact Submissions** will list messages if a form is ever added.

## 10. Spotting website problems

* **Overview** (the first page after signing in) shows whether the website
  is online and if anything is waiting to be published.
* **Website Health** runs checks and lists broken links or missing pictures,
  each with a **Fix** button that takes you to the right page.
* If the website itself seems down, contact your developer. Automatic
  "site is down" e-mails are not set up yet.

| Publish changes, restore old versions | | ✓ | ✓ |

the time.
